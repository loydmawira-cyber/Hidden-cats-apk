# Privacy Policy for Hidden Cats: Color Logic

Last updated: November 2026

Hidden Cats: Color Logic ("the Game") is developed as a privacy-respecting logic puzzle game.

### 1. Information Collection
- **Anonymous Gameplay Data:** The Game utilizes Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a randomized identifier for remote save synchronization and streak tracking. No personally identifiable information (such as real name, email, or telephone number) is collected or stored.
- **Local Data Storage:** Game history, board progress, and player preferences are stored locally on your device via Android Room Database and DataStore.
- **Crash Reporting & Diagnostics:** Anonymous diagnostics may be captured to detect and correct bugs and crashes.

### 2. In-App Purchases
All purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store payment method or credit card details.

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Players can reset all local progress at any time via Settings -> Reset Progress.

### 4. Children's Privacy
The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 5. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, please open an issue in the project repository.
