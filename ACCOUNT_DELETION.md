# Account and Data Deletion - Hidden Cats: Color Logic

Effective date: November 6, 2026  
Publisher: Smart Tech Labs  
Contact: smarttechlab.apps@gmail.com

Hidden Cats: Color Logic by Smart Tech Labs does not require a sign-in. Your game data is stored on your device and, when cloud features are active, against a random anonymous identifier. This page explains how to delete all of it.

## Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

1. Open Hidden Cats: Color Logic.

2. Go to Settings.

3. Under Data Management, tap "Delete My Data".

4. Read the warning and tap "Delete Everything".

Your progress, statistics, daily streak, and settings are removed from the device immediately, and a deletion of your anonymous cloud data is requested.

## Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email smarttechlab.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name of the app. If you have your anonymous Player ID, include it so we can find your data. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Puzzle history, statistics, and best times
- Daily challenge results and streaks
- Settings, hint balance, and local purchase status
- Cloud data stored against your anonymous identifier

## What is kept

- Purchase records held by Google Play. They belong to your Google account, so you can restore Premium and Remove Ads from the Shop afterwards. Hint packs that were already used are not restored.
- Data held by Google for ads and diagnostics, under Google's own policies.
- Minimal information we must keep to meet legal obligations or to prevent fraud, if any.

## Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling removes data stored on the device but may not remove cloud data. Use Option 1 first, or Option 2, if you want the cloud data deleted too.
